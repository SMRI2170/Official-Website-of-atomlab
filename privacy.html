Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Thank you for using Atom Lab. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user information within our application. By using our app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Atom Lab does not collect any personally identifiable information unless explicitly stated. However, the following data may be collected depending on your device settings and app usage:

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, language, and crash logs.
  • Usage Data: In-game progress, settings, and gameplay interactions.
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous data collected via third-party tools to improve app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Improving gameplay balance and user experience
  • Fixing bugs and identifying performance issues
  • Enhancing features and content
  • Ensuring app stability and security

3. Third-Party Services

Our app may use the following external services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • AdMob or other ad networks (if used)

These services may collect information as governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Advertising

If the app displays ads, ad partners may use device identifiers to show personalized or non-personalized ads. You can disable personalized ads in your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

We do not store personal data on our servers. Any gameplay data is stored locally on your device or through platform-approved services such as Google Play Games.

6. Children's Privacy

Atom Lab is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You may:

  • Reset app data from device settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads
  • Disable analytics (depending on device OS)

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. When changes occur, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
